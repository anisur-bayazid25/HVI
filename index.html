Explore relative vulnerability

The atlas brings together heat exposure, sensitivity and adaptive capacity across 7,706 grid cells and 134 ward areas. Higher HVI means greater relative vulnerability. Higher adaptive capacity means greater protection.

HVI classes run from Very Low (0–0.2) to Very High (>0.8–1). Other indicator maps group values into five bands within the selected spatial scale. Grid and ward scores are relative to their own scale.

Data sources and years

Land surface temperature: 2023. Population: WorldPop, 2020. Building footprints: Google Open Buildings, 2023. Informal settlements: 2016. Healthcare locations combine Survey of Bangladesh, OpenStreetMap and Google Maps. Roads are from OpenStreetMap. Road and healthcare collection dates vary and are not confirmed in the compiled layers.

Nearby healthcare

Choose road-network distance to see approximate paths to the nearest reachable facilities, or straight-line distance to compare proximity. Road routes follow mapped connections and available one-way tags. Dotted links show the connection from each selected location to the road network. These are estimates, not live navigation; traffic, closures, turn restrictions and current facility availability are not included.

The atlas is a guide to relative vulnerability, not a current heat forecast or a prediction of illness.
