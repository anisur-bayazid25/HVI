# Dhaka Heat Vulnerability Atlas

**Updated on: October 2026**

**[Explore the atlas](https://anisur-bayazid25.github.io/HVI/)**

An interactive view of heat vulnerability across Dhaka, with **7,706 grid cells**, **134 ward areas**, and **402 recorded healthcare facilities**.

## Explore

- Switch between grid and ward maps.
- View heat vulnerability, exposure, sensitivity, adaptive capacity and 13 individual indicators.
- Filter vulnerability classes or focus on the highest HVI decile.
- Search an area and explore its score, rank and indicator profile.
- Find the five nearest healthcare facilities by **road-network distance** or **straight-line distance**.
- Compare colored road routes, road distance, access-link distance and direct proximity. Filter facilities by type.
- Change basemaps, toggle markers and distance rings, download selected data and share a view.
- Use the atlas on desktop, tablet or mobile.

Road-network results are estimates based on mapped connections and available one-way tags. Dotted links connect selected locations to the road network. Traffic, closures, turn restrictions and current facility availability are not included. Road and straight-line rankings may differ.

## Data sources

Healthcare locations combine **Survey of Bangladesh, OpenStreetMap and Google Maps**. Roads come from **OpenStreetMap**. Other indicators include historical satellite-derived temperature, WorldPop population data, Google building footprints, poverty estimates and compiled environmental and settlement layers.

See [data sources and years](docs/DATA.md) and the [interpretation guide](docs/METHODOLOGY.md). Unknown collection years are identified rather than assumed to be October 2026.

Heat vulnerability is relative within each spatial scale. The atlas is not a current heat forecast or a prediction of illness.
